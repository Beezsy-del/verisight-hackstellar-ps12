# Forensic intelligence: screenshot and document tampering

Research date: 2026-11-13. This note supports the isolated tester, not a claim of validated forensic accuracy. Only primary publications, author-maintained repositories, official documentation, and author-linked dataset cards support factual claims below. No dataset or checkpoint was downloaded and no model was trained for this research. Proposed engineering choices are explicitly recommendations, not published benchmark results.

## What can improve now

Use bounded local residual and multiscale analysis to find candidate regions, compare those regions with similar nearby background/text, and require corroboration from a different evidence family before raising a strong manipulation finding. Recompression, scaling, sharpening, antialiasing and ordinary UI repetition must be negative controls. An authentic screenshot can have complicated compression artifacts; a carefully edited screenshot can have few surviving artifacts. A low anomaly score cannot establish authenticity.

Document text edits are especially challenging because they can occupy very small regions on consistent fonts/backgrounds. DocTamper's authors explicitly identify these differences from natural-image manipulation and use source-disjoint document subsets. Their presentation reports 170,000 generated document examples from 50,562 source documents. This supports retaining native-resolution crops and evaluating domain transfer, rather than treating a whole-image resize or natural-photo benchmark as sufficient. [Author CVPR presentation](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/media/cvpr-2023/Slides/21237.pdf).

Residuals are a plausible signal, not a universal detector. IMD2020's authors describe acquisition, format and manipulation artifacts and warn that insufficient diversity encourages overfitting to particular cameras/manipulations. A screenshot does not share the full camera-acquisition pipeline of a photograph. Consequently, photograph-trained residual models need separate screenshot evaluation. [Author-hosted Extended IMD2020 paper](https://library.utia.cas.cz/separaty/2021/ZOI/novozamsky-0541341.pdf).

Recent document-specific work investigates diverse JPEG quantization tables and reports architecture-dependent benefits, reinforcing the need to test actual compression diversity rather than one JPEG quality number. These are the authors' results, not results reproduced here. [DocQT author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19688).

## Evidence and implementation recommendations

1. Preserve the original bytes/hash, decoded dimensions, codec and available metadata; report processing limits. Keep original-coordinate bounding boxes. Work on downscaled images only for bounded candidate discovery, then inspect native-resolution regions when feasible.
2. Measure several recompression residuals, high-pass residual energy, local robust deviations and edge/stroke consistency. Compare like regions (similar luminance, edge density and text/background class). Normalize robustly and reject almost-empty patches; dividing by near-zero local variance creates spurious extremes.
3. Group JPEG/ELA/blockiness/recompression residuals into one compression family. Group related high-pass/noise measurements together. Multiple thresholds on the same residual are not independent witnesses. Cross-scale persistence improves stability but is not a separate independent vote. Require spatial agreement where two families claim the same local edit.
4. Distinguish global processing from localized inconsistency. Recompression, missing EXIF, a screenshot-shaped aspect ratio or an editor tag can explain history/context; none independently establishes malicious editing. Do not make document or screenshot classification itself incriminating.
5. For copy-move candidates, ORB/Hamming matching is inexpensive, but discard self/nearby matches, demand spatially separated clusters, verify a coherent affine transform with RANSAC and minimum inlier support, and compare transformed patches. Repeated letters/icons/table cells naturally match. A geometric duplicate is evidence of repetition, not proof of copying. OpenCV documents Hamming matching for ORB and robust geometric matching; the extra rejection logic is our proposed safeguard. [ORB matching](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/dc/dc3/tutorial_py_matcher.html), [geometric verification](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d1/de0/tutorial_py_feature_homography.html).
6. OCR should provide boxes, text, confidence and layout context for region selection. Low confidence, unfamiliar language, different fonts or inconsistent recognized numbers must not become automatic forgery verdicts. Semantic arithmetic checks should be labeled content-consistency observations.
7. Report unavailable, timed-out and not-applicable analyses distinctly from negative findings. Display heuristic scores as such; do not label an uncalibrated weighted sum a probability or a calibrated confidence.

## Learning-method decision

The following are engineering judgments, not measured rankings. The official scikit-learn guide describes boosting and random forests, including their model/compute tradeoffs. [Ensemble documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/ensemble.html).

| Approach | Feasibility and prerequisites | Decision for this tester |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GBDT over bounded regional/global features | CPU-friendly candidate for compact tabular evidence; requires enough licensed, diverse genuine/manipulated examples, source-group splits and held-out calibration. Can learn codec/domain shortcuts. | Prefer as a future trained fusion baseline once real labeled data exists. Retain feature provenance and missingness. No trained model is justified by a handful of synthetic fixtures. |
| Random forest | Convenient nonlinear baseline on the same features; depth/tree count control size and inference cost. Correlated features and class imbalance still matter. | Compare against GBDT under the identical split; no assumption it is automatically interpretable or calibrated. |
| Lightweight patch CNN | Can learn local texture/residual patterns, but patch labels and receptive-field/context choices matter. CPU inference may be practical after measuring target hardware; training/augmentation/data needs are larger. | Defer until rights and broad paired controls exist. Patch accuracy alone is not document-level false-positive control. |
| Localization network | Requires pixel masks, suitable checkpoints/licenses, tiling/merging and resolution-preserving evaluation. Domain shift and memory/latency are material. | Research candidate after permission and benchmark gates. DocTamper/STFL-Net paper results do not establish this application's accuracy, nor does a public repository automatically authorize weights. |

## Validation required before a stronger claim

The original WhatsApp pair is unavailable. Synthetic fixtures can verify implemented behavior and expose regressions, but cannot establish real-world screenshot accuracy or recovery of the reported failure.

Create paired clean/edited fixtures with exact masks; apply the same transformations to both: native PNG, JPEG quality sweep, repeated JPEG, downscale/upscale, small crop, blur/sharpen, text antialiasing and metadata stripping. Include naturally repeated text/icons, mixed font sizes, dark/light themes, multilingual text and clean flat backgrounds. Hold out entire source templates and transformations. Never split sibling crops or recompressions between train/test.

Measure genuine-image false-positive rate, sensitivity by edit size and operation, pixel/region localization overlap, abstention/missing-analysis rate and p50/p95 CPU latency. Report counts and failures with every rate; small synthetic samples do not justify precise general-population estimates. Compare old/new on identical bytes and disclose any retuned thresholds. For learned methods, add calibration curves, domain-disjoint test sets and genuine examples from each operational domain before deployment.

The immediate defensible improvement is more localized, inspectable evidence and fewer unsupported verdicts, backed by reproducible synthetic regression results. Real-data evaluation remains a separate explicit requirement; no SOTA, certainty, evidentiary admissibility or recovered-real-case claim follows from this note.
