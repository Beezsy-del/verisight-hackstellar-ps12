## What deliberately does not survive

B's fixed high-confidence labels, C2PA test-marker trust, oracle-fed offline feature flags, automatic statutory accusations, and existing weights as production classifiers are excluded. Their functionality was investigated: the model is genuine, but existing artifact inference does not control the headline verdict and its0.85 alert misses all historical test positives. Removing these claims is not removing proven detection value.

The corrected background-margin CIELAB implementation is already shared. Copying it again adds no scientific benefit. B's generator/schema/evaluation remain useful research patterns, but synthetic images cannot establish deployment accuracy. Research constraints and licenses are in RESEARCH_REFERENCES.md.
